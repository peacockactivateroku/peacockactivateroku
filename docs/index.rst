How to Install the Peacock App on Roku
======================================

If Peacock is not already installed:

1. Turn on your Roku device.
2. From the Roku Home screen, open **Streaming Channels**.
3. Search for **Peacock**.
4. Select the Peacock app.
5. Choose **Add Channel**.
6. Wait for the installation to complete.
7. Return to the Roku Home screen.
8. Launch the Peacock app.

How to Activate Peacock on Roku
================================

Follow these steps to activate your device:

1. Open the Peacock app on your Roku.
2. Select **Sign In**.
3. An activation code will appear on your TV screen.
4. Using another device, open a web browser.
5. Visit the official Peacock activation page.
6. Enter the activation code exactly as shown.
7. Sign in with your Peacock account if prompted.
8. Wait while the activation is verified.
9. Your Roku device will refresh automatically.
10. Start streaming your favorite content.

Sign In Directly on Roku
========================

Depending on your Roku model and the Peacock app version, you may also have the option to sign in directly using your Peacock email address and password instead of entering an activation code.

Common Roku Activation Problems
===============================

Activation Code Not Working
---------------------------

If your activation code isn't accepted:

* Verify that you entered the code correctly.
* Make sure the code has not expired.
* Restart the Peacock app to generate a new code.
* Enter the new code immediately.

Unable to Sign In
-----------------

If you cannot access your Peacock account:

* Verify your email address.
* Confirm your password.
* Reset your password if necessary.
* Ensure your Peacock subscription is active.

Peacock App Won't Open
----------------------

If the app won't launch:

* Restart your Roku device.
* Check your internet connection.
* Update your Roku software.
* Remove and reinstall the Peacock app if necessary.

Roku Does Not Refresh After Activation
--------------------------------------

If your Roku remains on the sign-in screen:

* Wait a few moments.
* Restart the Peacock app.
* Restart your Roku device.
* Generate a new activation code and repeat the activation process.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
=================================

Do I need an activation code every time I use Peacock on Roku?
--------------------------------------------------------------

No. The activation code is generally required only when signing in for the first time or after signing out of the app.

Can I use the same Peacock account on multiple Roku devices?
------------------------------------------------------------

Yes. You can sign in to your Peacock account on multiple compatible devices, subject to any simultaneous streaming limits associated with your subscription.

Why did my activation code expire?
----------------------------------

Activation codes are temporary for security purposes. If the code expires, reopen the Peacock app to receive a new one.

Can I install Peacock on all Roku devices?
------------------------------------------

Peacock is available on supported Roku players and Roku TVs. Availability may depend on your Roku model and software version.

What should I do if Peacock still won't activate?
-------------------------------------------------

Restart your Roku device, verify your internet connection, generate a new activation code, and ensure you're using the correct Peacock account. If the problem continues, consult the official Peacock Help Center for additional assistance.
